### Figures for the pitch (reproduce with `python tools/open_data_stats.py`)

- **12.1 million** traffic fines were issued in the UAE in 2016, **71%** of them for speeding (Bayanat, Cause of Traffic Fines).
- Dubai alone issued **4.6 million** traffic fines in 2016 (Bayanat).
- Dubai's official price list contains **2,377 different fines** from **23 government entities** (Dubai Pulse, Government Service Prices). That's how complex the system people have to navigate is.
- **672,163** vehicles were registered in the UAE in 2023 (Bayanat, Unified Federal Traffic System).
